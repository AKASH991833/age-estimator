Honest limits. Age estimation in the wild is approximate - this compact model is typically within about 5-8 years on clear frontal faces and worse on children, side angles, or low light. "Gender" here is a binary prediction of presentation (hair, features, styling) from pixels - it is not identity, and it can be wrong. Use it for demos and fun, never to judge or identify a person. All inference is local; nothing is uploaded.
